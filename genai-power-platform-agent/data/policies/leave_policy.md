# Annual Leave Policy

**Document owner:** People Team | **Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Applies to:** All Meridian Holdings employees (Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia offices)

## Entitlement

Full-time confirmed employees are entitled to **18 days of annual leave** per calendar year. Employees on probation accrue leave at the same rate but may only use accrued days after their first 3 months of service. Part-time employees accrue leave on a pro-rated basis according to their contracted hours.

Leave entitlement increases with tenure:
- Year 1–2 of service: 18 days
- Year 3–5 of service: 20 days
- Year 6+ of service: 22 days

## Applying for leave

All leave requests must be submitted through the HR Portal at least **3 working days** in advance for requests of 3 days or fewer, and **10 working days** in advance for requests longer than 3 days. Leave is approved by the employee's direct manager. Emergency leave (e.g. sudden illness in the family) may be applied for retroactively within 2 working days of returning to work, with a brief reason noted.

## Carry-forward and forfeiture

Employees may carry forward up to **5 unused leave days** into the following calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March or they are forfeited. Any leave beyond the 5-day carry-forward cap is forfeited at year-end and is not paid out, except where local employment law requires payout (this applies to Malaysia-based staff under the Employment Act).

## Unused leave on resignation

Employees who resign will be paid out for any unused, unforfeited annual leave as part of their final paycheck, calculated on their last drawn basic salary.

## Public holidays

Public holidays follow the gazetted list for the employee's home office location (Singapore, Malaysia, or Indonesia) and are published on the HR Portal each December for the following year.
