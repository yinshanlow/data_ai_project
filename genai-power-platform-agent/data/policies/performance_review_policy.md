# Performance Review Policy

**Document owner:** People Team | **Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Applies to:** All confirmed Meridian Holdings employees

## Review cycle

Meridian Holdings runs a **twice-yearly** performance review cycle: a mid-year check-in (June) focused on progress and coaching, and a year-end formal review (December) that determines ratings, bonus multipliers, and salary adjustments. Employees who joined within the last 3 months of a cycle are excluded from that cycle's formal rating.

## Rating scale

Year-end reviews use a 5-point scale: Exceptional, Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Partially Meets Expectations, and Below Expectations. Ratings are calibrated across each department by the Department Head and an HR Business Partner before being finalised, to reduce manager-to-manager bias.

## Self-assessment and manager review

Employees complete a self-assessment in the Performance Portal at least **1 week** before their scheduled review meeting. Managers complete their assessment independently and discuss both perspectives together with the employee during the review meeting, not before.

## Performance improvement plans (PIPs)

Employees rated "Below Expectations" are placed on a **60-day Performance Improvement Plan** with clear, measurable goals agreed jointly by the manager and HR Business Partner. Progress is reviewed at the 30-day mark. Failure to meet PIP goals by day 60 may lead to further HR action, up to and including termination, following local employment law requirements.

## Disputing a rating

Employees who disagree with their rating may raise a formal dispute with their HR Business Partner within **10 working days** of the review meeting. Disputes are reviewed by a panel of two HR Business Partners not involved in the original calibration.
