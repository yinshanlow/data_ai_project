# Business Travel Policy

**Document owner:** Finance Team | **Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Applies to:** All Meridian Holdings employees travelling for business purposes

## Booking travel

All flights and hotels for business travel must be booked through the approved corporate travel portal, TravelDesk. Bookings made outside TravelDesk will only be reimbursed at the equivalent TravelDesk rate, with any difference borne by the employee, except in documented emergencies.

## Approval thresholds

- Domestic travel (within the employee's home country): manager approval only.
- Regional travel (within Southeast Asia): manager approval plus Department Head sign-off.
- International travel outside the region: Department Head plus Finance sign-off, requested at least **2 weeks** in advance.

## Class of travel and accommodation

Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is permitted for flights over 6 hours for employees at Manager level and above. Hotel accommodation is capped at SGD 250/night for regional travel and SGD 350/night for international travel, unless the client site dictates a specific approved hotel at a higher rate.

## Per diem

A daily meal per diem of SGD 80 (domestic/regional) or SGD 120 (international) is provided in lieu of individual meal receipts while travelling, and does not require itemised proof — a signed per diem claim form is sufficient.

## Travel insurance

All business travel is automatically covered under the company's group travel insurance policy. Employees do not need to purchase separate travel insurance; a copy of the policy certificate is available on request from Finance for visa applications.
