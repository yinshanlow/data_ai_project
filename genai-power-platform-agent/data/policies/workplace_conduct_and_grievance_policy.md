# Workplace Conduct & Grievance Policy

**Document owner:** People Team | **Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Applies to:** All Meridian Holdings employees, contractors, and interns

## Expected standards of conduct

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and professionalism. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, or retaliation of any kind — whether in the office, on client sites, remote, or at company social events — will not be tolerated and may result in disciplinary action up to termination.

## Raising a concern

Employees who experience or witness misconduct can raise a concern through any of the following channels: their direct manager, their HR Business Partner, or the confidential Ethics Hotline (available 24/7, can be used anonymously). Employees are encouraged to use whichever channel feels safest — there is no requirement to go through a manager first, especially if the concern involves the manager.

## Investigation process

All formal complaints are investigated by an HR Business Partner not connected to either party, typically completing within **20 working days**. Both the complainant and the respondent are kept informed of the investigation's status (though not necessarily every detail, to protect confidentiality) and are entitled to bring a support person to any formal meeting.

## Non-retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith — even if the complaint is not ultimately upheld — is itself a disciplinary offence. This protection does not extend to complaints made maliciously or known to be false.

## Outcomes

Substantiated complaints may result in outcomes ranging from a documented warning to immediate termination, depending on severity, as determined by HR and, where relevant, Legal. All outcomes are documented and retained in line with the company's data retention schedule.
