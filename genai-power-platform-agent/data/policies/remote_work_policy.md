# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

**Document owner:** People Team | **Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Applies to:** All Meridian Holdings employees, excluding on-site client support roles

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period (3 months) and whose role does not require fixed on-site presence (e.g. front-desk, on-site client support, warehouse) are eligible for hybrid work. New hires still on probation work on-site full-time unless their manager grants an exception in writing.

## Standard arrangement

The default hybrid arrangement is **3 days in-office, 2 days remote** per week, with in-office days coordinated at the team level so core collaboration days overlap. Fully remote arrangements (5 days remote) require director-level approval and are reviewed every 6 months.

## Working from overseas

Employees wishing to work remotely from a country other than their home office location (e.g. a Singapore-based employee working from Malaysia for an extended period) must submit a Cross-Border Remote Work Request at least **2 weeks** in advance. Approval depends on tax, visa, and data-residency considerations and is capped at **20 working days per calendar year** unless an exception is granted by Legal.

## Equipment and connectivity

Employees on approved remote-work days are expected to maintain a stable internet connection and a private, secure workspace for calls involving client or company-confidential information. See the Expense Claims Policy for the monthly internet/mobile allowance available to remote workers.

## Core hours

Regardless of location, employees are expected to be reachable during core hours of **10:00–16:00** in their home office's local time zone, to support cross-team collaboration.
