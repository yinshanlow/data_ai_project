# Expense Claims Policy

**Document owner:** Finance Team | **Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Applies to:** All Meridian Holdings employees

## What can be claimed

Employees may claim reasonable, business-related expenses incurred while performing their duties, including:
- Client meeting meals (up to SGD 60 per person)
- Local transport for business purposes (taxi, ride-hail, public transport)
- Internet/mobile top-up for employees on approved remote-work arrangements (capped at SGD 40/month)
- Approved training courses and professional certification exam fees

## What cannot be claimed

- Personal meals or transport not related to a business purpose
- Alcohol, except at approved client entertainment events with prior Finance sign-off
- Fines, penalties, or traffic violations
- Any expense without a valid receipt or tax invoice

## Submission process

All claims must be submitted through the Expense Portal within **30 days** of the expense being incurred, with a scanned or photographed receipt attached. Claims submitted after 30 days require written justification and manager approval, and may be rejected at Finance's discretion. Claims under SGD 100 require only direct-manager approval; claims of SGD 100 or above also require Finance Team sign-off.

## Reimbursement timeline

Approved claims are reimbursed together with the next payroll cycle, typically within **10 working days** of final approval. Claims are paid into the employee's registered salary bank account — Finance does not process reimbursements to third-party accounts.

## Corporate cards

Employees issued a corporate card must reconcile all transactions in the Expense Portal by the 5th of the following month. Failure to reconcile for two consecutive months may result in the card being suspended pending review.
