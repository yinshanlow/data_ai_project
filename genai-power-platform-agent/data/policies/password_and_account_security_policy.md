# Password & Account Security Policy

**Document owner:** IT Security Team | **Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Applies to:** All Meridian Holdings employees and contractors

## Password requirements

Corporate account passwords must be at least **12 characters**, containing a mix of upper- and lower-case letters, numbers, and symbols. Passwords must be changed every **90 days** and cannot reuse any of the last 5 passwords. Shared or generic passwords (e.g. team-wide logins for a single tool) are prohibited except where explicitly approved by IT Security for service accounts.

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

MFA is mandatory for all accounts accessing email, the Company Portal, VPN, and any system holding client or financial data. Employees must enrol a second factor (authenticator app preferred; SMS as fallback) within **3 days** of account creation. Accounts without MFA enrolled after this window are automatically locked pending IT Security review.

## Reporting a suspected compromise

Any suspected phishing email, unauthorised login, or lost/stolen device must be reported to the IT Security Team **immediately** via the emergency Service Desk line, not the standard ticketing queue. IT Security will force a password reset and review account activity within 1 hour of the report during business hours.

## Device security

Company laptops must have disk encryption and endpoint protection enabled at all times — both are enforced automatically and cannot be disabled by the employee. Employees must lock their screen when stepping away from their workstation, including in the office.

## Third-party tools

Employees may not use personal (non-corporate) cloud storage, email, or messaging apps to store or transmit company or client data. Approved third-party tools are listed in the Software Catalog on the Company Portal.
