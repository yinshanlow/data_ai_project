# Parental Leave Policy

**Document owner:** People Team | **Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Applies to:** All confirmed Meridian Holdings employees, in addition to statutory entitlements in each home office country

## Maternity leave

Confirmed female employees are entitled to **16 weeks of paid maternity leave**, which may begin up to 4 weeks before the expected delivery date. This is inclusive of, and tops up, any statutory government-paid maternity leave the employee is entitled to in their home office country (Singapore, Malaysia, or Indonesia), so the employee does not receive less than 16 weeks paid in total.

## Paternity leave

Confirmed male employees are entitled to **4 weeks of paid paternity leave**, to be taken within 12 months of the child's birth. Paternity leave may be split into two blocks of at least 1 week each, subject to manager approval on timing.

## Adoption leave

Employees who adopt a child under 12 months old are entitled to the same leave entitlement as biological parents (16 weeks for the primary caregiver, 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver), starting from the date the child is placed in their care.

## Requesting parental leave

Employees should notify their manager and HR at least **8 weeks** before the expected leave start date where possible, to allow for cover planning. A shorter notice period is accepted for adoption placements or premature births where advance notice isn't possible.

## Returning to work

Employees returning from maternity or adoption leave (primary caregiver) may request a temporary flexible or reduced-hours arrangement for up to 3 months upon return, subject to role feasibility and manager agreement.
