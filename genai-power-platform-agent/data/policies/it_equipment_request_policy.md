# IT Equipment & Laptop Request Policy

**Document owner:** IT Team | **Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Applies to:** All Meridian Holdings employees

## Standard issue equipment

New employees are issued a standard laptop (Windows or macOS, employee's choice at offer stage), a headset, and a docking station on their first day. Standard laptop refresh cycle is **every 3 years**, tracked automatically by the IT Asset Management system.

## Requesting new or additional equipment

Requests for equipment beyond the standard issue (external monitors, ergonomic keyboards, a second laptop for a specific project) must be submitted via the IT Service Desk ticketing system with manager approval attached. Standard requests are fulfilled within **5 working days**; requests requiring new procurement (equipment not already in stock) may take **10–15 working days**.

## Loss, theft, or damage

Any lost, stolen, or damaged equipment must be reported to the IT Service Desk within **24 hours** of discovery. Theft must also be reported to local police where the incident occurred, with the police report number provided to IT for insurance purposes. Replacement of equipment lost or damaged through employee negligence (e.g. left unattended in a public place) may be charged back to the employee's department at IT's discretion, prorated against the asset's remaining depreciation value.

## Software installation

Employees may self-install software from the approved Software Catalog in the Company Portal without additional approval. Software not in the catalog requires a request through the IT Service Desk, which will be reviewed for licensing and security compliance before installation — typically a **2–3 working day** turnaround.

## Offboarding

All company equipment must be returned to IT (or a designated courier arranged by IT for remote employees) on or before the employee's last working day. Final pay may be withheld pending equipment return, in line with the employment contract.
