# New Hire Onboarding Process

**Document owner:** People Team | **Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Applies to:** All new Meridian Holdings employees

## Before day one

HR sends a welcome pack 5 working days before the start date, including the employment contract, a first-day itinerary, and instructions for setting up the Company Portal account. IT provisions the employee's corporate email, laptop, and system access (see IT Equipment & Laptop Request Policy) 2 working days before the start date, based on the New Hire Provisioning Form submitted by the hiring manager.

## Day one

New hires attend a half-day orientation session covering company overview, code of conduct, benefits enrolment, and IT/security basics. The direct manager is responsible for a first-day team introduction and assigning an onboarding buddy from within the team.

## First 30 days

- **Week 1:** Complete mandatory compliance training (data protection, workplace conduct, anti-harassment) via the Learning Portal.
- **Week 2:** Manager sets initial 30-60-90 day goals with the new hire.
- **Week 4:** HR check-in call to gather early feedback on the onboarding experience.

## Probation period

All new hires serve a **3-month probation period**. A confirmation review is conducted in the final two weeks of probation, based on manager feedback and goal achievement. Probation may be extended by up to 3 months in cases where performance is inconclusive; extension requires HR Business Partner sign-off.

## System access

Access to specific systems beyond the standard onboarding set (e.g. finance systems, client data platforms) must be requested separately by the manager through the IT Service Desk, following the principle of least-privilege access.
